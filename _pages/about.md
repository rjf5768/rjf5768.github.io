I'm a master's student in Computer Science at UMD, advised by Professor [Yizheng Chen](https://surrealyz.github.io/index.html).

Previously, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from The Pennsylvania State University, where I was advised by Professor Suman Saha. My undergraduate research focused on the intersection of large language models and program analysis, resulting in two preprints exploring whether LLMs can simulate symbolic execution and recover program semantics from obfuscated code.

Feel free to reach out to me!

Research Interests
======

My research interests broadly lie in artificial intelligence. I am currently focused on code intelligence and large language models for program analysis, but I am also interested in:

- Reasoning and verification in AI systems
- AI agents and autonomous systems
- Trustworthy and reliable AI
